Cookie Policy

How EnvSave uses cookies, local storage and third-party resources on envsave.com.

Last updated: __LEGAL_UPDATED__

We keep this short: we use only the cookies needed to sign you in, plus Google Fonts if you allow it. No analytics, no advertising, no cross-site tracking.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as localStorage, work the same way. Some are strictly necessary for a site to work. Others need your consent under the EU ePrivacy Directive, the GDPR and Spain's LSSI (Art. 22.2).

2. Strictly necessary

These are needed to provide the service you request, such as signing in to your dashboard. They don't require consent and can't be disabled in our settings.

NameProviderPurposeDuration
__sessionClerk (envsave.com)Short-lived token that keeps you signed in~1 minute, refreshed automatically
__client_uatClerk (envsave.com)Records when you last signed in or out, to keep the session in sync1 year
__client, __cf_bmClerk (clerk domain)Authentication session and bot protection on Clerk's serversSession to 1 year
cookie-consent (localStorage)EnvSaveRemembers your cookie choice and when you made itUntil you clear it or change it

Clerk cookies are only set after you open the sign-in form or visit the dashboard.

3. Functional (consent required)

ResourceProviderPurposeData shared
Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com)GoogleDisplays the Inter and JetBrains Mono typefacesIP address, user agent, referring page

Google Fonts doesn't set cookies, but loading it sends your IP address to Google, so we only load it after you agree. If you decline, the site uses your system fonts and works the same.

4. What we don't use

We don't use analytics cookies, advertising or retargeting pixels, social media trackers or fingerprinting. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

5. Managing your choice

You can change or withdraw consent at any time from the cookie settings (also linked in every page footer). You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop sign-in from working.
