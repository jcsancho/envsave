What personal data EnvSave processes, why, and the rights you have over it.

2. What data we process

Local CLI and libraries

The envsave CLI, the Node library and the Python bridge store your secrets in an encrypted file on your own computer (~/.envsave/vault.enc). This data is never sent to us unless you run a cloud command such as envsave backup or envsave sync.

Account data (only if you sign up)

  • Identity data: email address and an internal user ID. Sign-in is provided by Clerk, which may also process your name, profile picture and sign-in method if you use a social login.
  • License data: your license token and its creation and expiry dates.

Encrypted vault backups (only if you use cloud backup)

When you run envsave backup or envsave sync, your vault file is uploaded already encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your password on your machine. We store the encrypted file, its size, a SHA-256 checksum and timestamps. We do not have your password and cannot read your secrets.

Technical data

  • Server logs: when you visit the website or the CLI contacts our API, our web server processes your IP address, request time, requested URL and user agent to deliver the service and protect it against abuse.
  • Cookies and local storage: see our Cookie Policy.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and license data: while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days.
  • Encrypted vault backup: only the latest backup is kept; each upload replaces the previous one. It is deleted together with your account.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days, unless needed to investigate an abuse or security incident.
  • Consent record: stored in your browser until you clear it or change your choice.

We may keep some data longer where the law requires it.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted ("right to be forgotten");
  • restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that happened before. For cookies, use the cookie settings.

To exercise these rights, email __LEGAL_EMAIL__ from the address linked to your account. We reply within one month. You may also complain to a data protection authority. In Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

8. Security

Secrets are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before any upload, all traffic uses HTTPS, and license tokens are signed server-side. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong vault password and keep your license token private. Report vulnerabilities via a GitHub security advisory.

9. Children

EnvSave is a developer tool and is not intended for anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect data from children.
